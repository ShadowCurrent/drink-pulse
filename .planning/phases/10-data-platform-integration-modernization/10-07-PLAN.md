<interfaces>
HealthService (@MainActor) serializes write, update, remove and removeSample per event uuid through runSerial. Authorized path per op: performWrite queries sampleUUID(forEventUUID:) then saves when none exists; performUpdate deletes the cached sample then performWrite; performRemove deletes the cached sample (or queries) and clears the cache.
HealthWriting is a Sendable protocol (isHealthDataAvailable, requestAuthorization, authorizationStatus, save, sampleUUID(forEventUUID:), delete). FakeHealthStore implements it with samplesByEvent and counters and is final.
HealthKitAdapter today: `final class HealthKitAdapter: HealthWriting, <unchecked qualifier> Sendable` with stored `let store = HKHealthStore()`, `let alcoholType = HKQuantityType(.numberOfAlcoholicBeverages)` and `let gramsPerStandardDrink = 14.0`.
ReminderService and WeeklySummaryService accept an injected NotificationScheduling center and a UserDefaults suite; FakeNotificationCenter (ReminderServiceTests.swift) exposes addedRequests, removedBatches and pendingIds.
HealthWriteHooksTests.withWriteEnabled is `async rethrows`; its closure at line 116 does not throw, so the leading try is redundant and compiles with a warning.
</interfaces>
