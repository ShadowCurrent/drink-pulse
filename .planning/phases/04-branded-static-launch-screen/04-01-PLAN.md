<objective>
Replace the auto-generated blank iOS launch screen with a branded static launch
screen: the app icon (flattened from `AppIcon.icon`) centered on a background
that exactly matches `Color(.systemBackground)`, wired through the platform's
`UILaunchScreen` mechanism -- no animation, no text, no spinner (LAUNCH-01,
D-01 through D-04).

Purpose: cold launch currently shows a blank, auto-generated screen
(`INFOPLIST_KEY_UILaunchScreen_Generation = YES`); this phase makes the first
thing a user sees feel native and branded, with zero change to app startup
timing or logic.

Output: two new Asset Catalog entries (`LaunchBackground.colorset`,
`LaunchIcon.imageset`), an updated `project.pbxproj` (or, if the primary path
is unworkable, a documented standalone `Info.plist` fallback) wiring
`UILaunchScreen` to those assets, and a regression UI test guarding normal app
launch.
</objective>
